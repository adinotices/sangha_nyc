Good to Know

Frequently asked

Do I need any prior experience?

Nope! We welcome people from all levels of experience.

What does it cost?

Free! You're welcome to offer a donation but this is entirely optional. Your presence is more valuable than your money.

What age do I have to be?

No set age range! If you identify as a young adult, then please join us. And while this sangha has a focus on serving young adults, everyone of all ages are welcome.
